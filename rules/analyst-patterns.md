# Analyst pattern rules

A named investor is a reading pattern, not a data source.

- Apply the pattern only to a metric the engine already emitted, and name the metric and its period.
- Luiz Barsi Filho: trailing dividend or FII distribution. The three-month sample is not his long horizon. Do not compute a ceiling price.
- Décio Bazin: report the realized dividend yield. Do not grade it against a fixed hurdle.
- Warren Buffett and Benjamin Graham: drawdown is the sample path. Multiples are current statistics. Intrinsic value, owner earnings, moat, and margin of safety are not calculated.
- FII card: yield and P/VP when present; vacancy only for a brick fund. Together they are not a buy rule.
- Lyn Alden: keep the crypto series apart from the headline. Andreas M. Antonopoulos: explain and do not instruct. On-chain metrics, stock-to-flow, and published price targets stay out.
- Never copy an analyst's portfolio, quote, or target into the report.

# Risk disclosure rules

Every report includes the educational disclaimer. Equity, crypto, and FII paths include a class-specific risk sentence. Scenarios include the assumption disclosure. Short samples and missing indicators are listed as uncertainties.

A long-horizon lens (Barsi, Buffett) states that the calculated window is the received sample, about three months for the daily series, not that horizon. A scenario shock remains an assumption and is not the outcome of the lens.
